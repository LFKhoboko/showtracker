📸 SHOWTRACKER SCREENSHOTS
============================

Drop 2–8 PNG screenshots here (1080×1920 portrait) for the Play Store / App Store listing.

Recommended screenshots:
  - home.png       — Home page with trending and recently aired
  - shows.png      — My Shows with filter pills
  - search.png     — Search page showing results
  - recommend.png  — For You recommendations page
  - calendar.png   — Activity calendar with dots
  - stats.png      — Stats page with genre chart

How to take them:
  1. Open ShowTracker on your phone (Add to Home Screen first)
  2. Take screenshots with your phone's screenshot button
  3. Transfer them to your computer
  4. Rename them as above and save here

Then update manifest.json screenshots array with the correct filenames.
