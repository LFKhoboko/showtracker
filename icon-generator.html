🎨 ShowTracker Icon Generator

Generate PNG app icons for Play Store & App Store submission

📐 1. Choose size & download

📋 2. What to do with these icons

  1. manifest.json — The references are already set to /icons/icon-192.png and /icons/icon-512.png
  2. Save the downloaded files into your TVTime/icons/ folder
  3. Google Play Store needs the 512×512 as the app icon and a 1024×1024 for the feature graphic
  4. Apple App Store needs the 1024×1024 version

🚀 3. Generate Android App with PWABuilder

  1. Go to pwabuilder.com
  2. Enter your PWA URL (use ngrok to expose localhost, or deploy to GitHub Pages)
  3. Click "Package for Stores" → "Android"
  4. Download the generated .aab (Android App Bundle)
  5. Upload to Google Play Console → Create new app → Production
